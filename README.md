# Baim's English Lab

English for a five year old getting ready for primary school.
Vocabulary follows the Cambridge Pre A1 Starters and A1 Movers wordlists.
302 words and 152 questions. Single file, no audio folder.

## Publish
Upload `index.html` to a repo, then Settings > Pages > main / root.
Family photos are embedded; the voices come from the device.

## Progression
| Level | What it trains | Format | Choices |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Words: animals & family | hear a word, tap the picture | 3 |
| 2 | Words: food, body, home, school, doing | same, harder distractors | 5 |
| 3 | Words: places, world, jobs, feelings, opposites | same, same-topic distractors | 6 |
| 4 | Listening: what, where, who, how many | spoken question, 3 picture answers | 3 |
| 5 | Talking: what do you say? | a real situation, pick the reply | 3 |
| 6 | Thinking: riddles and clues | several clues, work out the answer | 3 |

From level 2 the wrong pictures come from the SAME topic, so a lion sits next to
a tiger and a zebra. Shape matching stops working; he has to know the word.

Level 5 is conversation: "Your friend says Happy birthday. What do you say?",
"Papi says it is raining. What do we take?", "The light is red. What do we do?"

Level 6 needs reasoning from several clues at once:
"I have wings but I cannot fly. I swim and I love ice. What am I?"
These are read a little slower and he gets 20 seconds before a nudge.

## No guessing
Two wrong taps and he must find the right one twice in a row.
Choices reshuffle after every tap. A wrong option never shares the answer's picture.

## Voices
Device voices only. English for the question, Bahasa Indonesia for the meaning
after a correct answer. Add the Indonesian voice on iPad under Settings >
Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices. The meaning always appears on the card.
